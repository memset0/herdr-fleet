## Why

Sends on the lead keep failing with `probe_failed`, `unconfirmed`, `modal_cue`, `changed`, `mismatch` and `draft_present`, and a `probe_failed` that leaves the probe character in the box turns the next send into `draft_present`. The receipts under the state directory's `sends/` hold hashes and words only, and the lab's examples under `surface/examples/` are masked and kept only for a sent probe or a refusal over an unreadable modal. Neither keeps the screens a failing decision was made on, so the operator cannot pin a root cause after the fact. The owner approved a local-only diagnostic that keeps them.

## What Changes

- When a Send Protocol request ends in one of the screen-decided failures, Fleet keeps the unredacted screens that decision read (S0, S1, S2 and the last two reads, as the multiplexer returned them, styling included) with the receipt, in one file per failure under a fixed, server-owned folder of the Collie state directory.
- The folder is owner-only, files are owner-only and written atomically, a symlinked folder or file is refused, file names are generated by the server, and retention is bounded by count and total bytes.
- Keeping the screens never changes a send's outcome or timing: the write happens off the send's path, and its errors are swallowed and logged once.
- Nothing new is served, shown or sent anywhere: no route, no UI, no wire field.

## Non-goals

- No change to any Send Protocol decision, bound, key or receipt field.
- No viewer, export, upload or crew-link transfer of the kept screens; the operator reads the files on the machine.
- No change to Collie's counters, lab examples or request files.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `fleet-send-diagnostics`: a failed send's screens kept locally for root-cause work.

### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact

Baseline Collie v1.23.3. Reused upstream behavior: the Send Protocol's steps, reads and receipts are Collie's, unchanged. Fork-owned: `fleet/send-failure-screens.ts` (policy, writer, retention) and its test. Upstream-owned ports: `bridge/surface/protocol.ts` (each read keeps its raw text and step; one optional dependency called after a receipt settles) under the existing `patient-send-protocol-port`, and `bridge/index.ts` (one import, one dependency line wiring the sink to the state directory) under a new invasive entry. The bridge runs the code, so a release carrying it is a MINOR.
