## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A failed send keeps the screens its decision read
When a Send Protocol request on a pane with a profile settles with a reason decided by reading the screen (`draft_present`, `not_free`, `probe_failed`, `unconfirmed`, `mismatch`, `changed`, `modal_cue`, `no_evidence` or `dialog_after_enter`), or settles refused, `typed_not_submitted` or `unknown` on a screen whose modal read `unreadable` or `unknown`, Fleet SHALL keep one record of that failure on the bridge's own machine. The record SHALL hold the settled receipt (request id, harness, version, profile id, outcome, reason, keys and timings), the time it was kept, and each distinct read the decision had (S0, S1, S2, and the last two reads), every one as the multiplexer returned it, unredacted and with its styling, labelled with its role and the step that made it. A send that settles `sent`, `effective` or `discarded`, a refusal decided before any read, and a blind send on a pane with no profile SHALL keep no record.

#### Scenario: A probe that fails
- **WHEN** a send is refused `probe_failed`
- **THEN** one record is kept holding the receipt and the S0 and probe reads, raw

#### Scenario: Debris turns the next send into a draft refusal
- **WHEN** a send is refused `draft_present` because the box holds text
- **THEN** one record is kept holding the receipt and the S0 read that showed the box

#### Scenario: A send that goes in
- **WHEN** a send settles `sent`
- **THEN** no record is kept

### Requirement: Kept screens stay private, bounded and local
Records SHALL be written only under one fixed folder inside the Collie state directory, created owner-only (0700), each record owner-only (0600) and written atomically through a temporary sibling. The folder SHALL be refused, and nothing written, when it is a symbolic link or not a folder, and no write SHALL follow a symbolic link. A record's file name SHALL be generated by the bridge and never derived from a value a client supplied. The folder SHALL keep at most a fixed number of records and a fixed total of bytes, removing the oldest first, and each kept screen SHALL be cut to a fixed size. No route, interface, notification or crew-link field SHALL expose the records.

#### Scenario: Many failures
- **WHEN** more failures are kept than the count bound allows
- **THEN** the oldest records are removed until the count and byte bounds hold

#### Scenario: A symbolic link where the folder should be
- **WHEN** the folder's path is a symbolic link
- **THEN** nothing is written through it and the failure is logged once

### Requirement: Keeping screens never changes a send
Keeping a record SHALL NOT change any send's outcome, reason, keys, receipt or timing: the record SHALL be written after the receipt settles and off the request's path, and an error while keeping it SHALL be swallowed and logged at most once per bridge run.

#### Scenario: The disk refuses the write
- **WHEN** writing a record fails
- **THEN** the send's answer is the same as without the diagnostic, and one warning is logged
